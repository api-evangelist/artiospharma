Low dose alnodesertib plus gemcitabine met the primary endpoint of progression-free survival (PFS) versus gemcitabine alone in patients with platinum-resistant high-grade serous ovarian cancer Study provides additional proof of concept that combining alnodesertib with DNA-damaging agents in high replication stress cancers delays tumor progression Phase 2 studies with higher dose alnodesertib plus irinotecan are ongoing…
